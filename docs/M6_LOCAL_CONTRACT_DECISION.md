## Accepted contract decisions

1. **Association model:** M6 creates two independent, durable histories: `shipment_container_assignments` and `shipment_voyage_assignments`. A shipment may have multiple containers, but a container may belong to at most one shipment during any overlapping assignment interval within an organization. A shipment has at most one voyage association during any overlapping interval. Multiple shipments may be associated with one voyage. M6 does not create an independent container-to-voyage contract: a voyage association shown for a container is contextual through its shipment and only for intersecting assignment intervals. No assertion that a container physically boarded a vessel is implied.
2. **Time representation:** Every assignment has an immutable, explicitly UTC `startsAtUtc` and an optional `endsAtUtc`. Interpret the interval as half-open `[start,end)`, with a null end treated as unbounded future. End must be strictly greater than start; equal endpoints and reversed windows are rejected. Adjacent windows do not overlap. Historical and future-dated records are permitted and do not automatically transition based on the wall clock.
3. **Lifecycle and version:** Create an open or already-bounded assignment. A created assignment is immutable except for its single permitted closure of a previously null end, supplied with exact positive `expectedVersion` and explicit UTC end. Closing increments its version once; no re-opening, editing parents/start, deleting rows, or silently replacing existing assignments. Corrections are represented by a new, non-overlapping record with documented provenance. A duplicate create is a conflict, not an idempotent overwrite.
4. **Overlap enforcement:** Enforce non-overlap in PostgreSQL, not just preflight application reads, using tenant-scoped exclusion constraints over half-open `tstzrange` and `btree_gist` support. Container allocation exclusion key is `(organization_id, container_id)`; voyage allocation exclusion key is `(organization_id, shipment_id)`. Transactional retries must not produce duplicate or overlapping assignments. The migration must fail safely if the extension or exclusion constraint cannot be provisioned; never silently downgrade to application-only checks.
5. **Ownership and parents:** All assignment foreign keys are composite `(organization_id,id)` references to existing M4 containers/voyages and M5 shipments, with restricted deletion. Create only when shipment, container and voyage (as applicable) are Active and belong to the session-selected organization. Existing closed historical associations remain readable when a parent is later Archived. Neither tenant identifiers nor archived identifiers become reusable.
6. **Voyage route and schedule:** A new shipment-voyage link requires exactly matching origin/destination M3 port UUIDs and a voyage with `voyageStatus` Planned or Underway. M6 supports a single direct voyage per assignment window, not multi-leg route composition. If both shipment and voyage have complete planned UTC windows, reject known disjoint windows; unknown individual timestamps do not imply a fabricated schedule. Do not mutate the shipment workflow or voyage progress automatically.
7. **Parent lifecycle:** An open assignment blocks archival of its shipment/container/voyage, cancellation or completion of that shipment, and terminal voyage cancellation or arrival while the relevant voyage link remains open. Close the affected associations first. These checks must be atomic with the parent mutation and backed by database enforcement for direct SQL, including concurrent changes. Closed associations do not block archival. Other M4/M5 behavior remains unchanged for records with no open links.
8. **Authorization:** New typed `ViewShipmentAssignments` permission for Analyst, Manager and narrowly read-only Auditor. `ManageShipmentAssignments` for Analyst and Manager. Administrator and Support receive no implicit permission. Auditor read projections may include scoped shipment/container/voyage labels, but may not call existing M4 catalog-management endpoints. Analyst can allocate resources despite lacking M4 catalog write permission.
9. **HTTP/API:** Add assignment routes below the selected-organization shipment path; strict typed JSON, same-origin CSRF, bounded pagination, sanitized 400/401/403/404/409/503 with correlation IDs and `Cache-Control: no-store`. Support create, scoped list/detail and versioned closure only. Do not add hard-delete, public APIs or bulk assignment actions.
10. **Concurrency:** Use the accepted organization, session, account, membership and parent lock/revalidation discipline. Serialize same-container and same-shipment races and cross-parent archive conflicts. All actions commit assignment state, version and provenance atomically, or roll back completely. A failed request must never leave one side of an implied association committed.
11. **Audit semantics and future milestones:** Retain immutable assignment history, actor and UTC timestamps. It is M6 operational allocation history, not M8 tracking-event history, M10 status projection or M16 generalized audit. Do not add event ingestion, automatic tracking updates, independent equipment interchange, registry ownership claims, advanced exports or multi-leg planning.
12. **Verification and Q1 checkpoint:** G6 requires real .NET/PostgreSQL/HTTP/browser verification, mutation and concurrency probes, M0-M5 regressions, scans, outage/recovery, isolated E:-backed evidence and independently reviewed raw results on one exact source SHA. At G6 only, attempt a read-only clone/restore of a representative retained historical PostgreSQL snapshot in disposable storage. If none exists, record `NOT APPLICABLE` with the discovery method and reason. Never touch the only retained volume. Dedicated accessibility testing stays at M24-04.

## Accepted alternatives and boundaries

- **Direct vs multi-leg voyage:** This contract accepts exact single-leg route matching in M6. Leg-sequenced itineraries require a separate future design, not an implicit relaxation.
- **Closing vs deletion:** This contract preserves associations after closure and never permits physical deletion. Historical correction uses a new record, not rewriting old rows.
- **Container-voyage semantics:** A container's displayed voyage follows the intersecting shipment association. Do not add a separate authoritative container-voyage table without revising this contract and its consistency rules.
- **Parent terminal policy:** This contract requires explicit closure before a terminal/archival operation. No automatic closure or inferred timestamps.
