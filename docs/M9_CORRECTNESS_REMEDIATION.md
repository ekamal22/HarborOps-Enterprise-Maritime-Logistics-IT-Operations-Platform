## Source audit findings

The ten findings in the [follow-up source audit](M9_CORRECTNESS_AUDIT_20261104.md) were source-level. This campaign executed their corresponding behaviors: CA-01 and CA-06 (restricted worker role processes real work and cannot forge attempts or bypass redrive), CA-02 (compiles), CA-03 (standalone G9 stack renders and runs on its own ports), CA-05 (isolated-schema migration and claim), CA-09 (tenant quota and two-process barrier tests), CA-10 (readiness probe fails on a missing grant and recovers). CA-04 (concurrent reference change) and CA-08 (the redrive recheck of the session security version) are exercised by deterministic race tests and by mutation families 13 and 14.
