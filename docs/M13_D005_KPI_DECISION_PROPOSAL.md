# M13 D-005 Operational KPI Decision Proposal

**Date:** 2026-11-08
**Status:** PROPOSED, NOT APPROVED
**Accepted source:** main at eb529368a2447061fc1838cd3967b0ffedae669d
**Decision owner:** Explicit project policy approval required before M13-01 closure or M13 application implementation.
**Scope:** FR-012, FR-002, NFR-007, NFR-008. This document does not authorize G13 acceptance, deployment, migrations or a change to accepted M9-M12 rules.

## 1. Source facts and invariants

- M5 has shipments with workflow_status Draft, Booked, InTransit, Arrived, Completed or Cancelled, record_status Active or Archived, planned departure/arrival UTC timestamps, and append-only shipment_status_changes. The latter records workflow transitions, not independently observed physical transport.
- M6 has shipment_voyage_assignments with starts_at_utc and nullable ends_at_utc, scoped to the owning organization. M12 selects current associations at its reference instant.
- M10 has shipment_tracking_input_versions and shipment_tracking_projections with input_revision, correction_generation, policy_version and observed_state. A projection can be absent or stale. The projector is disabled by default, ETA remains null.
- M11 uses m11-rules-v1 for EquipmentConflict, ArrivalScheduleOverdue, RecordedArrivalLate and DepartureSlippageRisk. Both arrival grace and departure tolerance are 60 minutes, with strictly-greater-than comparisons. Condition Active/Inactive/Unknown/Stale is distinct from lifecycle Open/Acknowledged/Resolved. A resolved RecordedArrivalLate can remain factually Active, and recurrent occurrences have independent IDs.
- M12 search returns live pages and a reference asOfUtc. It is not a frozen historical shipment snapshot and cannot serve as a KPI reconciliation token.
- M9 sustained ARRI and DEPA transport-event processing is prohibited. Those events remain NeedsReconciliation under the accepted 10,000 unresolved-work cap. No KPI may describe parked events as movements or derive predictive ETA from them.

## 2. Proposed policy version and dashboard vocabulary

Adopt **m13-kpi-v1**, with a versioned catalog, UTC-only windows, explicit source classes and no user-configurable formulas. Copy the active policy version and a stable filter digest into each answer and evidence record. Changing a numerator, eligibility, event interpretation or tolerance requires a new decision and version.

Source classes: **CURRENT_WORKFLOW** means M5 rows at one transaction snapshot; **RECORDED_WORKFLOW_EVENT** means accepted M5 status transition history; **CURRENT_EXCEPTION** means current M11 condition/lifecycle/evaluation checkpoint, not a replayed historical state; **CURRENT_TRACKING_QUALITY** means M10 version match or absence, not a physical position. Where an authoritative historical reconstruction cannot be made, the API must refuse a historical current-state request rather than guess.

All counts are tenant-local. A shipment counts once in a shipment-count numerator, regardless of multiple exception occurrences. Exception-occurrence counts instead count occurrence IDs. All joins include matching organization_id and resource ID, never a public reference alone.

## 3. Proposed catalog and exact definitions

| ID | Display name | Numerator, denominator and source | Eligibility, exclusions and interpretation |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| K01 | Active shipments | Count M5 shipments where record_status=Active and workflow_status is not Completed or Cancelled; denominator is all currently unarchived shipments | Includes Draft and Arrived, excludes Archived; current operational backlog, not an event history |
| K02 | Workflow status distribution | One current M5 record per status; denominator is all unarchived shipments | Six mutually exclusive statuses. Archived appears as a separate record-status total, not a seventh workflow status |
| K03 | Completed workflows in period | Count distinct shipment IDs with first to_status=Completed transition at or after fromUtc and before beforeUtc; denominator not applicable | Includes subsequently archived records because this is a historical recorded transition. Do not equate with delivered cargo |
| K04 | Cancelled workflows in period | Count distinct shipment IDs with first to_status=Cancelled transition in window; denominator not applicable | Includes later archive; cancellations are not successful throughput |
| K05 | Strict on-time recorded arrivals | Count completed-in-window shipments with first M5 Arrived transition at or before their applicable recorded planned-arrival reference; denominator is completed-in-window with a present plan and Arrived transition | Strict on-time means no positive lateness. Missing plan/Arrived evidence excluded from the fraction and separately counted. Do not claim physical arrival |
| K06 | Within 60-minute recorded-arrival tolerance | Same eligible K05 denominator; count first M5 Arrived transition no later than planned arrival plus approved M11 60-minute grace | Distinct from strict K05. Equality passes; after by one PostgreSQL microsecond fails |
| K07 | Recorded late arrivals | Count distinct currently unarchived, M11-eligible shipment IDs with latest evaluated RecordedArrivalLate condition Active; denominator is eligible evaluated shipment IDs | Includes Resolved occurrences whose factual condition is still Active; label as recorded workflow lateness, not confirmed real-world delay |
| K08 | Schedule overdue, arrival unconfirmed | Count distinct currently unarchived eligible shipments with current ArrivalScheduleOverdue condition Active; denominator is eligible evaluated shipments | Schedule-only unconfirmed delay. Do not combine with K07 under a label implying verified arrival |
| K09 | Departure slippage risk | Count distinct currently unarchived eligible shipments with current DepartureSlippageRisk condition Active; denominator is eligible evaluated shipments | At-risk headline. An M5 InTransit event late versus plan and future scheduled arrival is a heuristic, not ETA |
| K10 | Equipment observation conflicts | Count distinct currently unarchived eligible shipments with current EquipmentConflict condition Active; denominator is eligible evaluated and fresh-M10 shipment IDs | Requires accepted matching M10 input revision, correction generation and tracking policy. Stale/unknown is not conflict-free |
| K11 | Open exception occurrences | Count M11 occurrence IDs with lifecycle Open or Acknowledged on currently unarchived eligible shipments; denominator not applicable | Separate from factual condition. Never count each history entry as another occurrence |
| K12 | Exception occurrence trend | Number of occurrence IDs first_detected_at_utc in each UTC bucket, grouped by rule and initial opening; denominator not applicable | Historical opening events, regardless of later resolution or archive. This is not a point-in-time reconstruction of historical Open/Resolved status |
| K13 | Evaluation coverage | Numerator eligible shipments with all required M11 rule checkpoints present for the current source revision; denominator active-record non-Draft/non-Cancelled shipments | Display percent plus uncovered count; stale and unassessed records are not treated as risk-free. Coverage is a separate diagnostic metric |
| K14 | Unassessed or stale sources | Count distinct eligible shipment IDs with no matching M11 checkpoint, version drift, missing/obsolete M10 projection where that rule needs it, or an explicit Unknown/Stale state; denominator eligible shipments | Reasons are separated into missing evaluation, stale evaluation, missing evidence and stale projection. A shipment appears once in headline, possibly several reasons in detail |

**Plan amendment rule:** K05/K06 compare the first recorded Arrived transition against the planned arrival value associated with that workflow fact, not an arbitrary current amended plan. M5 status history does not store past schedule values, and accepted source permits schedule edits in Draft/Booked only. Implementation must prove by source constraints and a populated fixture that the plan is immutable before an Arrived transition for every eligible record. If this cannot be proven for imported/legacy data, mark its reference plan unknown and exclude that row from K05/K06, or add an explicitly approved versioned plan-history mechanism before claiming historical on-time accuracy. Do not reconstruct missing history from updated_at_utc.

**Current status and archival:** Current snapshots include only selected-organization rows as visible at transaction time. Archived and Cancelled rows are excluded from K07-K11 current operational risk and from K01, but archived historical transition facts remain included in K03/K04/K12. A later restore affects future current snapshots, not earlier event counts. A future historical as-of status view is not part of v1.

**Duplicated/corrected data:** M8 duplicate receipts and M9 processing attempts are not throughput. M10 corrections supersede, rather than multiply, observed equipment evidence. A current M11 condition is attributed once per shipment/rule, selecting the current authoritative evaluation/occurrence, not all historical occurrences; K12 intentionally counts separate genuine occurrence IDs. Identical source replay must not add metric members. New correction generation invalidates freshness until evaluated. Manual M5 transition is counted once by unique shipment plus first matching status.

## 4. UTC, snapshot and window contract

- All instants are UTC ISO 8601 with explicit Z or +00:00; reject offset-less, date-only and non-zero-offset timestamps. Apply half-open [fromUtc,beforeUtc), strictly from inclusive and before exclusive. Reject reversed, equal or single-ended intervals; recommend maximum span 366 days, matching M12 where filters overlap.
- Recommend default reporting window trailing seven complete UTC days ending at the most recent 00:00 UTC; user-selected windows are explicit. Never infer local timezone from a browser display. Daily buckets start at 00:00 UTC, weekly buckets at Monday 00:00 UTC. The last partial bucket is visibly marked if permitted.
- Current metrics K01/K02/K07-K11/K13/K14 describe a **fresh transactional snapshot** and use database-provided evaluatedAtUtc plus policy/version and freshness metadata. Historical metrics K03-K06/K12 use accepted event facts in the requested window, evaluated against that same snapshot. An arbitrary historical asOfUtc supplied by a client must not rewrite current mutable values.
- Recommend one PostgreSQL REPEATABLE READ transaction per overview build, using a common source snapshot and separately captured database UTC evaluation instant. Revalidate selected organization, live user/session/security version, active membership and typed dashboard permission while holding scoped locks until snapshot materialization commits. Handle serialization retries with bounded attempts. The UTC timestamp labels computation, not a guarantee that PostgreSQL MVCC has a wall-clock-perfect rewind point.
- **Recommendation for exact cross-request drill parity:** persist a short-lived, server-owned report snapshot and its minimal metric member keys within the aggregation transaction. Bind an opaque protected report token to actor, session, selected organization, filter hash, catalog version, snapshot ID and expiration. Recommend 10-minute expiration, no renewal without recomputation, one active report per principal/organization and a bounded per-tenant row budget determined by benchmarks. On expiry return 409 report_expired; never silently switch to live M12 search.
- Drill pages read ONLY stored snapshot members, use a deterministic (metric ID, bucket, shipment ID, occurrence ID) keyset, and reauthorize live identity, membership, organization and read-only grant on every request. Snapshot columns contain only scoped IDs, metric memberships, bucket and safe display summaries. No raw tracking payload, actor details or free-form reasons. Current shipment detail links always revalidate and may have changed since the snapshot. Label such differences clearly.
- If durable report materialization exceeds approved resource caps, return a bounded failure without partial counts. Do not create an unbounded PostgreSQL transaction or pretend a capped sample is a complete total. An alternate live-only overview is permitted only with an explicit policy revision stating that drill pages can drift.
- No cache shared across tenants, sessions or roles. Use Cache-Control: private, no-store and clear state on account, membership, organization or role changes. Expired reports and membership rows require measured cleanup, retention and quota tests.

## 5. Nulls, ratios and precision

Whole-number counts are nonnegative integers. A denominator of zero yields null ratio with a visible Not enough data state, never 0% or infinity. Fractions are computed from exact integer counts, returned as numerator and denominator, then rounded half-up to two percentage decimal places only for display; underlying data remains exact. Unknown is not zero. Include excluded counts and reason labels for K05/K06 and freshness coverage. No imputation or ETA inference.

If evaluator is disabled or lags, render K07-K11 with an explicit stale/partial coverage indication; do not show a headline 0 as definitive risk-free state. K10 must carry the M10 policy/input-version freshness evidence. Exception trend K12 is labelled occurrence openings, never active risk at historic dates.

## 6. Reproducible reference fixture and expected results

Use isolated tenant A with twelve shipments S01-S12 and tenant B with two colliding-reference decoys. Capture a database UTC anchor T after fixture insertion, define W=[T-7 days,T), and place first completion transitions for S01, S02, S03 in W; S04 completes before W. Place one cancellation transition for S05 in W. S03 is subsequently Archived. Make all other records Active. The active workflow statuses at query time in tenant A are S06 Draft, S07/S11 Booked, S08/S09/S12 InTransit, S10 Arrived, S01/S02/S04 Completed and S05 Cancelled.

M11-eligible (record Active, not Draft/Cancelled) shipments are S01, S02, S04, S07, S08, S09, S10, S11 and S12. Evaluate all four current rules for the first eight from consistent source revisions; intentionally leave S12 unevaluated for the coverage test. S02 has a recorded arrival more than 60 minutes late and a human-resolved but still Active RecordedArrivalLate occurrence. S03 has a recorded arrival 30 minutes after its plan but within the 60-minute tolerance; S01 recorded arrival is at/before plan. S07 has active ArrivalScheduleOverdue (no recorded arrival); S08 has active DepartureSlippageRisk and a future scheduled arrival; S09 has fresh accepted M10 EquipmentConflict; the others have nonactive current rule conditions. Set S07 occurrence Acknowledged, S08/S09 Open, S02 Resolved. Ensure S12 has missing schedule/projection data so no positive risk is inferred. Tenant B has one Completed-in-W and one overdue Booked record that must be excluded entirely from A.

Expected tenant A values: K01 **7**, K02 **11 unarchived** with Draft 1, Booked 2, InTransit 3, Arrived 1, Completed 3, Cancelled 1 and Archived total 1; K03 **3**; K04 **1**; K05 **1/3 = 33.33%**; K06 **2/3 = 66.67%**; K07 **1**; K08 **1**; K09 **1**; K10 **1**; K11 **3 occurrences**; K13 **8/9 = 88.89%**, with one unevaluated. K12 source-opening expected counts must equal the deliberately seeded four occurrence IDs (S02, S07, S08, S09), attributed to their configured buckets, regardless of current resolution. K14 at least one missing-evaluation shipment S12, with any additional unknown/stale reason asserted against the exact fixture truth table rather than concealed in an aggregate.

Negative controls: move S03 arrival from +60 minutes to +60 minutes plus one microsecond, move a completion to exactly beforeUtc, archive S07, change M10 correction generation for S09, duplicate S08 occurrence history, and revoke the session between overview and drill. Recompute all impacted expectations explicitly. The integration fixture must ensure the relative-to-T event and cutoff spacing is large enough not to cross a boundary during a run.

## 7. Alternatives and requested approval

| Choice | Recommended default | Alternative and tradeoff |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Source authority | M5 recorded workflow plus current rule-versioned M11 and fresh M10 evidence | Treating transport receipts as movement would violate the active M9 hold |
| Arrival tolerance | Strict K05 plus separate M11-aligned 60-minute K06 | One combined on-time number obscures contractual and operational distinctions |
| Delay/risk | Split recorded-late, schedule-unconfirmed, departure-risk and equipment-conflict | Collapsing into one delayed count falsely suggests physical confirmation |
| Reporting consistency | Repeatable-read build plus scoped materialized report members for exact drill parity | Live drill queries cost less storage but may differ under concurrent changes |
| History | Event-window counts plus current-state metrics, with unsupported historical current-state requests rejected | Historical status reconstruction requires additional retained state/versioning |
| Evaluator lag | Unknown/partial display with coverage and freshness | Treating missing evaluations as zero creates false operational assurance |
| Access | Dedicated dashboard-read permission; Analyst/Manager default, Auditor explicitly granted read-only, Support/Administrator denied | Reusing broad shipment-read permission would implicitly grant an unapproved dashboard privilege |

**Approve or revise D-005 explicitly**: m13-kpi-v1 catalog and labels; 60-minute comparison semantics; strict on-time denominator; archived/cancelled/history handling; seven-day defaults and 366-day maximum; current versus historical boundary; ten-minute report snapshots and member retention/quotas; freshness treatment; exact fixture expectations; typed Auditor read-only grant model and relevant privacy/performance ceilings. This document is a recommendation, not approval. If approval changes the catalog, reconcile the implementation and G13 fixture contracts before writing code.
